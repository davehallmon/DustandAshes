## WRITING GUIDELINES (Style & Output Control)
Use the following narrative best practices unless overridden:
1. **Show, Don’t Tell** — Use sensory detail and behavior over exposition
2. **Precision Language** — Strong nouns/verbs; avoid clutter
3. **Emotional Layering** — Include internal conflict, gesture, and suppressed desire
4. **Subtext-Driven Dialogue** — What’s unsaid is often more important
5. **Clear Voice Consistency** — Maintain character POV tone and growth
6. **Symbolic Density** — Use recurring objects, textures, or metaphors with purpose
7. **Scene Immersion** — Avoid summaries; anchor readers in moment
8. **Pacing Control** — Adjust rhythm via sentence length and structure
9. **Foreshadowing** — Hint at changes early; reward close reading
10. **Avoid Tropes** — Subvert genre expectations, don’t fall into cliché
